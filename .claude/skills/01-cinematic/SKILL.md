## The 2-Second Hook Framework

**THE CRITICAL PRINCIPLE:** In cinematic storytelling, you have exactly 2 seconds to stop the scroll, catch the eye, and demand sustained attention. This is the difference between viral and invisible.

### The Hook Table: 10+ Cinematic Techniques

|---|---|---|

## Cinematic Prompt Philosophy

### Camera Language: 15+ Techniques

**1. Establishing Shot**: "Camera positioned 50 feet from subject. Wide focal length (24–35mm). Pan slowly revealing scale, three distinct depth layers."

**2. Push-In / Dolly Forward**: "Camera dolly forward 3 feet at constant speed over 2 seconds. Maintain shallow depth of field. Subject centered."

**3. Pull-Back / Reveal**: "Camera pulls back 20 feet over 3 seconds. Background reveals environmental scale. Subtle lens breathing."

**4. Whip Pan**: "Whip pan left to right in 0.5 seconds. Motion-blur trails permitted. No jarring stop, smooth deceleration."

**5. Handheld / Steadicam**: "Handheld camera. Micro-vibrations: 0.5–1mm frame jitter. Breathing motion 2–3 pixels. NOT locked-off rigid."

**6. Tracking Shot**: "Camera tracks subject from side profile. Subject at frame-right, environment at frame-left. 3 mph equivalent."

**7. Crane Up**: "Camera rises vertically 40 feet over 4 seconds. Reveals landscape as crane rises. Slight tilt down."

**8. 360 / Orbital Spin**: "Camera orbits 270 degrees around subject over 5 seconds. Constant 10-foot distance. Counterclockwise."

**9. Rack Focus**: "Rack focus from foreground (2 feet) to background (30 feet) over 1.5 seconds. f/1.4 cinema equivalent."

**10. Dutch Angle**: "Frame tilted 25 degrees counterclockwise. Horizon line diagonal across frame throughout."

### Lighting Theory: 10+ Cinematic Setups

**Three-Point**: "Warm key 45° left, soft fill 60% intensity right, rim at 80% intensity behind subject."

**Chiaroscuro / Noir**: "90% shadow, 10% illumination. Hard-edged light. Single source. Mid-tones nearly absent."

**Golden Hour**: "Warm amber-orange at low 15° angle. Diffused by atmospheric haze. Warm color temp 3200K."

**Cool Blue / Moonlight**: "Cool blue 6000K+. Low-angle directional. Shadows blue-tinted. Minimal intensity. Eerie mood."

**Volumetric / God Rays**: "Light passes through particle-filled atmosphere. Visible rays. Dust motes. Spiritual mood."

**Firelight**: "Warm orange-red 1800K from visible fire. Flickering 2–4 Hz. Large dancing shadows."

### Color Grading

**Teal & Orange**: "Shadows biased cyan-teal (200° hue), highlights orange-gold (30°). Saturation 110%."

**Desaturated Accent**: "Overall saturation 30%. One accent color [red/gold/cyan] at 100%."

**Golden Nostalgia**: "Color temp 3200K amber-gold. Saturation 115% on warm tones."

**Bleach Bypass**: "Lift black point. Greys elevated. Grain 150%. Vintage analog."

**Cyberpunk Neon**: "Saturation 140%. Split-toning: shadows cyan, highlights magenta-pink. Glow on lights."

## 5 Large Example Prompts

### EXAMPLE 1: Film Noir Detective

```
[HOOK 0–2s] Black screen silence. At 0.8s: cool blue light explosion from
frame-left. Hard 5000K industrial fixture creates extreme hard-edged shadow.

[CONTEXT 2–4s] 1940s noir warehouse interior. Camera 40 feet back, low angle
15°. 35mm equivalent. Chiaroscuro lighting: hard 3000K key upper-left at
60°, minimal fill (15%). Film grain, 35mm stock.

[ACTION 4–8s] Slow dolly forward at 1.5 ft/s. Detective silhouette
materializes in shadow-pool center-frame. At 5.5s: eye glint. At 6s:
cigarette pulled from pocket.

[CLIMAX 8–10s] At 8s: lighter ignites, reveals weathered face. At 8.3s:
desk lamp flicks on. Two-source conflicting shadows. At 9.5s: fade to black.

[SPECS] f/2.0, focus locked on face, 1.5 ft/s constant, 10s total, 16:9.
[GRADE] Bleach bypass noir: 0% saturation, grain 150%, cold blue shadows.
[AUDIO] 0–1s silence. 1–4s jazz trumpet (-6dB). 8–8.3s lighter scratch.
```

### EXAMPLE 2: Epic Landscape Aerial

```
[HOOK 0–2s] Extreme reverse macro on water droplet refracting landscape.
At 0.5s: whip-pull backward explosively. At 1.2s: extreme wide desert
vista. Scale shock at 2s.

[ACTION 4–10s] Crane descent from 200ft at 3 ft/s. Valley floor details

90° pan left revealing mountain.

[CLIMAX 10–15s] 10–12s: canyon reveal. 12–14s: music crescendo. Sun touches
mountain-top, backlight creates silhouette terrain lighting. 14–15s:
pull-back reveals human figure in valley.

[SPECS] 28mm ultra-wide, f/5.6 deep focus, 15s total, 16:9.
[GRADE] Golden hour 3200K amber. Saturation 120% on golden areas.
[AUDIO] 0–2s: wind. 2–4s: strings (-4dB). 10–12s: brass swell (+2dB).
```

### EXAMPLE 3: Dramatic Dialogue Close-Up


### EXAMPLE 4: High-Speed Chase

```
[HOOK 0–2s] Black silence 0–0.8s. At 0.8s: explosive percussion hit.
Whip-cut to high-speed movement. Subject enters frame at 45+ mph equivalent.
Motion blur 20%.

[ACTION 4–9s] Subject at high speed, camera tracks at 4-foot distance,

[SPECS] 35mm, f/4–5.6, 3.5 ft/s base → 5+ ft/s peak, 12s total.
```

### EXAMPLE 5: Tender Emotional Farewell

