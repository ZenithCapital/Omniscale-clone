## 3D CGI Prompt Philosophy

### Render Styles

- **Pixar/Stylized**: Warm saturated colors, smooth shading, exaggerated proportions. "Pixar-style 3D render, Disney animation aesthetic."
- **Photorealistic**: Pixel-perfect physical simulation, accurate materials. "Photorealistic 3D render, 8K quality, near-photographic realism."
- **Low-Poly**: Geometric faceted forms, flat colors. "Low-poly 3D model, geometric faceted style, flat-shaded."
- **Claymation**: Organic handmade aesthetic, visible deformation. "Claymation aesthetic, plasticine texture, stop-motion 3D."
- **Cel-Shaded**: Comic book aesthetic, bold outlines. "Cel-shaded rendering, graphic novel style, bold outlines."
- **Isometric**: Perfect 30–45° perspective, geometric clarity. "Isometric 3D view, 45-degree perspective."
- **Wireframe**: Skeleton-only geometry, neon glowing lines. "Wireframe 3D, neon wireframe aesthetic, technical blueprint style."

### Material Science & Surface Properties

- **Subsurface Scattering (SSS)**: Light penetrates translucent surfaces (skin, wax, marble). "Fine facial SSS showing blood flow beneath skin."
- **PBR**: Real physics, metallic/roughness workflows. "Physically accurate PBR materials, accurate Fresnel reflections."
- **Metallic Finishes**: Polished (mirror), brushed (directional), oxidized. "Mirror-polished steel with perfect specular highlights."
- **Glass & Crystal**: Clear, frosted, colored. "Crystal clear glass with sharp Fresnel reflections and chromatic aberration."
- **Fabric**: Woven texture, dynamic wrinkles. "Soft velvet fabric with rich SSS in highlights."
- **Fur & Hair**: Individual strand detail. "Dense fur with individual strand detail and SSS in backlighting."
- **Water**: Caustics, refraction, surface tension. "Crystal clear water with dancing caustics on the bottom."
- **Skin**: Pore detail, SSS, color variation. "Highly detailed human skin with visible pores and natural SSS."

### Lighting Setup Language

- **HDRI**: 360° environment lighting. "Lit by outdoor HDRI with warm golden-hour light."
- **3-Point**: Key, fill, back. "Professional 3-point: warm key, cool fill, crisp rim."
- **Caustics**: Light refracting through water/glass. "Dynamic caustics dancing across the surface."
- **God Rays**: Visible light beams through dust/fog. "Dramatic god rays streaming through dust particles."
- **Global Illumination**: Bounce light, color bleeding. "Rich GI with color bleeding from surrounding objects."
